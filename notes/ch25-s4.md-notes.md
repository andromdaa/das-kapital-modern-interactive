# Notes for ch25-s4.md

1. ch25-p060 – First technical use of "pauperism" in Ch. 25 ("the swamp of pauperism"). – Added the §7 wording in parentheses at that point; no second explanation in p062. – §7.0. Marx's p062 description of the "sphere of pauperism" develops the term, but p060 comes first and does not define it.

2. ch25-p066 – First appearance of "relative surplus-value" in Ch. 25. – Added the §7 wording in parentheses after "the production of relative surplus-value". – §7.0; Marx only refers back to Part IV here, he does not define it.

3. ch25-p066 – "agony of toil slavery" (MECW and other editions read "agony of toil, slavery"). – Kept as printed. – A missing comma is not a stray mark, and "toil slavery" can be read as a compound, so it has more than one reading (§1.4).

4. ch25-p066 – "i.e.*,* on the side of" has an italic comma. – Rendered "that is, on the side of" with a plain comma. – One-reading markup slip (§1.4).

5. ch25-p056 – "*teste* England" (Latin tag in Marx's voice, not on the §3.4 list). – Rendered "as England bears witness". – §2.3: set phrases in Marx's voice get plain English; not a famous tag.

6. ch25-p056 – "a complaint of the want of hands" (no quotation marks in the source). – Rendered "complaints of a shortage of hands", keeping the word "hands" without adding scare quotes. – It is the employers' complaint (n. 21 quotes "for want of hands"); §3.1 allows "hands" where Marx echoes the employers' word, and §3.3 forbids adding quotation marks.

7. ch25-p056 – "where machinery enters as a factor, or where only the modern division of labour is carried out" – kept the comma after "great workshops" so the "where" clauses can apply to the automatic factories as well as the workshops. – Keeps the original's scope open (§1.1).

8. ch25-p059 – "the premium that the exploitation of children sets on their production" – kept "their production" (= producing children) rather than spelling it out. – Spelling it out is the natural reading, but the phrase is short and kept close to the original to avoid any shift; reviewer may plainify to "on producing them".

9. ch25-p062 – "*faux frais*" in Marx's voice. – Rendered "overhead costs". – §3.4.

10. ch25-p063 – "the lazarus layers of the working class". – Kept the image ("Lazarus layers") and added the neutral identification "(named after the beggar Lazarus in the Bible)". – §1.1(c): the image depends on a reference a modern reader may not know.

11. ch25-p063 – "consolidated surplus population" – rendered "a fixed, settled surplus population". – Plain sense of "consolidated" here (permanently established); no strengthening intended.

12. ch25-p066 – Long colon-introduced list after "We saw in Part IV." split into separate sentences. – Each item remains Marx's own claim, as in the original; the "We saw" frame covers the first sentence and the rest follow as his restatement. Logged because the split loosens the grammatical link to "We saw".

13. ch25-p068 – Ortes quotation contains Italian parentheticals with printed oddities ("all, istessa" for "all'istessa"; "alia" for "alla"). – Kept the Italian exactly and added literal bracketed translations: [economic good and evil in a nation always at the same measure], [the abundance of goods in some always equal to the lack of them in others]. – §2.3. The oddities are in foreign text, which is copied exactly.
