# Notes: ch25-s2.md

Format: paragraph ID – the problem – what I decided – why.

1. ch25-p017 – "productivity of labor" is a first appearance; p017 is its first technical use, although p019 then describes how the degree of productivity is expressed. – Added the §7 wording in a parenthesis at p017. Added nothing in p019. – p017 comes first and does not define the term itself; p019 describes how productivity is measured in a society, which the §7 wording does not contradict.
2. ch25-p018 – "the increase of stock" inside Smith's quotation: a modern reader may take "stock" as company shares or inventory. – Kept the word and added "[capital]". – §2.2: gloss, don't replace. In Smith's usage "stock" means accumulated wealth used as capital, which Marx's lead-in paragraph also assumes.
3. ch25-p019 – Three first-appearance terms in one sentence: "division of labor", "manufacture", "labor process". – Put the division of labor and manufacture explanations in a short parenthetical pair of sentences right after the sentence, and worked the labor process explanation into the next sentence. – §7.0 allows "a short sentence right after"; putting all three inline would have made the example sentence unreadable.
4. ch25-p019 – "with the same tension of labour power". – Rendered "with the same exertion of labor-power". – "Intensity" would bring in the separate §7 term "intensity of labor", which Marx does not use here.
5. ch25-p019 – "or in the diminution of the subjective factor ..." – Kept "or" (an alternative wording of the same point) rather than "that is". – "That is" would make the equivalence stronger than Marx states it.
6. ch25-p020, p022 – "per cent." – Rendered "percent" (American usage in Marx's voice). Numbers kept as digits. – Earlier rewrites use both forms; §1.5 asks for American spelling in Marx's voice.
7. ch25-p023 – "primitive accumulation" is a first appearance, but Marx names and defines it right here ("It may be called primitive accumulation, because it is the historic basis ..."). – No §7 explanation added. – §7.0 "Never contradict the text": Marx defines the term in this passage, and the §7 wording (peasants driven off the land) would add content he explicitly postpones ("How it itself originates, we need not here inquire as yet").
8. ch25-p023 – "how it is only upon this supposition that division ... can be organised ...; how instruments ...; how huge natural forces ...; and how the transformation ..." It is unclear whether "only upon this supposition" governs the later "how" clauses as well. – Kept the single long sentence with the parallel "how" clauses, and placed "only on this condition" in the first one only, as in the original. – §1.2 ambiguity beats sentence length; splitting would force one reading.
9. ch25-p023 – Long relative clause "where the means of production are the property of private persons, and where the artisan therefore ..." – Turned into two statements ("On the basis of ... the means of production are the property of private persons. So the craftsman ..."), keeping "therefore" as "So". – Keeps the inference Marx makes; no new link added. "artisan" → "craftsman" (glossary handicraftsman → craftsman).
10. ch25-p023 – "cooperation" first appearance. – Added the §7 wording in a parenthesis at "cooperation on a large scale". – First technical use in the chapter.
11. ch25-p024 – "concentration" (of capital) is a first appearance, but Marx develops the concept in this very paragraph ("this kind of concentration which grows directly out of, or rather is identical with, accumulation"). – No §7 explanation. – §7.0 "Never contradict the text": his own words are the definition. The earlier "concentration on a vast scale" in p023 refers to means of production, not the technical term.
12. ch25-p024 – "in such proportion as they form aliquot parts of the total social capital". The glossary renders "aliquot part" as "equal fraction", but here the capitals are not equal; "equal fractions" would suggest that every individual capital is the same size. – Rendered "grow in proportion to the fraction of the total social capital that each forms". – Following the glossary verbatim would change the meaning; fidelity outranks (§1.1). Logged as a deviation from §3.3.
13. ch25-p025 – "centralization" is a first appearance, but Marx defines it here ("This is centralisation proper, as distinct from accumulation and concentration"). – No §7 explanation. – §7.0 "Never contradict the text".
14. ch25-p025 – "expropriation" first appearance; the §7 wording ends "(here, land and means of production)", which is wrong for this passage (capitalists expropriating capitalists). – Used the first part of the §7 wording with a grammatical adjustment: "(expropriation means taking away people's property, here one capitalist's by another)". – §7.0 allows grammatical adjustment; the original "here" clause would contradict the text.
15. ch25-p026 – "The cheapness of commodities demands, caeteris paribus, on the productiveness of labour". "demands ... on" does not parse; the only reading is "depends on". – Corrected to "depends on". – One-reading typo (§1.4); the sentence continues "and this again on the scale of production", which only fits "depends".
16. ch25-p026 – "Modern Industry" first appearance. – Added the §7 wording in a parenthesis. *caeteris paribus* → "Other things being equal" (§3.4).
17. ch25-p026 – "antagonistic capitals". – Rendered "rival capitals". – §3.2 keeps "antagonism" for class antagonism; here it means competing capitals.
18. ch25-p027 – "But if the relative extension and energy ... is determined, in a certain degree, by ..., progress in centralisation does not ... depend ...": a concessive "if". – Kept the "if ..., ..." structure rather than "although" or "may be". – Keeps the strength: Marx grants the dependence "in a certain degree" without either asserting or hedging it further.
19. [^12] – Engels' editorial note. – Kept the "*Note in the 4th German edition.*" heading and "*F. E.*" signature as printed; split the one sentence into two. – §2.6: editorial notes stay attributed.
