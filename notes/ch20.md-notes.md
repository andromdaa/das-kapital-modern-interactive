4. ch20-p007 – "only 6 or 8 hours, he receives ... only 2s. or 1s. 6d. a day": at 3d. an hour, 6 hours = 1s. 6d. and 8 hours = 2s., so the amounts are listed in reverse order. – Kept as printed. – Not a single-character typo; reordering would be "correcting" the text (§1.4).
