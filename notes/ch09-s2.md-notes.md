# Notes for ch09-s2.md

1. ch09-p036 – "he produces in 8 hours 13⅔ lbs." At 1⅔ lbs. per hour, 8 hours give 13⅓ lbs., and p025/p026 say 13 1/3 lbs. for the same cotton portion. – Kept "13⅔" as printed and did not correct it. – §1.4 forbids correcting arithmetic or facts; the figure is not a pure one-character typo with only one possible reading (it could be a source or transcription error), so it stays and is logged for the reviewer.

2. ch09-p030 – "the equivalent for the value of the labour consumed, or the 3s. variable capital". Strictly, variable capital is the value of labor-power, not of "labour". – Kept "the value of the labor used up"; did not change it to "labor-power". – Changing it would correct Marx/the translator and resolve the wording; the glossary keeps "value of labor" as printed.

3. ch09-p036 – "very barbarian notions", "love of lucre", "sycophant doctrinaires", "incident of historical celebrity". – Rendered "very barbarous notions", "the love of gain", "fawning doctrinaire theorists", "the following historically famous incident". – Plainer words with the same sneer; "fawning" follows the bible's own rendering of "sycophantic" in §6(b). "doctrinaire" kept as an adjective so the sense of rigid, theory-bound defenders is not lost.

4. ch09-p036 – Who speaks: the manufacturer's claim ("it shows, he will say, ...") and the "barbarous notions" of the interested parties (the list of what the spinner supposedly produces in each period, ending with the "last hour") must not read as Marx's own view. – Kept the tag "he will say", kept "Such people may get it into their heads that ..." governing the whole list in one sentence, and kept the scare quotes on "last hour". The "double miracle" sentence keeps Marx's irony ("the poor spinner") without explanation.

5. ch09-p028 – "although ... yet when viewed in this connexion" – Kept as one sentence with "although ... yet" rather than splitting, so the concession and the main claim stay tied; "connexion" → "connection" (American spelling, §1.5).

6. ch09-p034 – The original is one long infinitive sentence ("To split up in this manner the product ... ; to do this, is ... no less important than it is simple"). – Split into: a statement that the product can be split this way, three sentences for the three parts, then "To do this is no less important than it is simple, as will be seen later on ...". – All three parts, their "only", and the claim about later application to "complicated and hitherto unsolved problems" are kept; the split does not resolve any ambiguity.

7. ch09-p029, p031, p036 – Missing full stops after "lbs" ("4 lbs of yarn", "those 4 lbs", "20 lbs of yarn", "16 lbs", "4 lbs", "1⅔ lbs"). – Kept as printed. – Abbreviation punctuation inconsistency, not a meaning issue; left untouched to stay close to the source.
