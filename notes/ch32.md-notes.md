# Notes for ch32.md

1. ch32-p001 – "primitive accumulation" first appears in the opening sentence, where Marx defines it himself ("i.e., its historical genesis", then "it only means the expropriation of the immediate producers"). – No §7 explanation added for "primitive accumulation". – §7.0: never add the explanation where Marx is defining the term in that very passage.

2. ch32-p001 – "expropriation" first appears in the same sentence, immediately defined by Marx ("i.e., the dissolution of private property based on the labour of its owner"). – No §7 explanation added. – Same rule as note 1; Marx's own "i.e." clause is the definition.

3. ch32-p001 – "capital" first appears (technical sense) inside the question "What does the primitive accumulation of capital … resolve itself into?". – Added the §7 wording as a separate parenthetical sentence right after the question rather than inside it, so the question stays a question and its structure is not broken. – §7.0 allows "a short sentence right after".

4. ch32-p001 – "cooperation" and "division of labor" first appear inside one long list of what petty production excludes. – Kept the list intact and put both §7 explanations in a parenthetical right after that sentence. The cooperation wording ("under one capitalist's command") is kept verbatim although the passage concerns pre-capitalist production; it describes the term, not this mode of production, so it does not contradict the text. – Putting two long glosses inside the list would have made the key sentence unreadable.

5. ch32-p001 – "the control over, and the productive application of the forces of Nature by society": "by society" grammatically governs both nouns. – Rendered "the control over the forces of Nature and their productive application by society", which keeps "by society" attached to the pair as in the original without spelling out a reading. – Ambiguity kept (§1.1).

6. ch32-p001 – Context file lists "concentration of capital", "accumulation of capital", "wages" and "classical political economy". The only matches are "concentration of these means of production" / "socially concentrated", "primitive accumulation of capital" (handled in note 1), "wage labour(ers)" and "its adequate classical form" — none is the §7 technical term. – No explanations added. – §7.0 word-match caveat. The heading "Capitalist Accumulation" is kept as printed and not glossed.

7. ch32-p001 – "pigmy property of the many". – Rendered "dwarf-sized property of the many", keeping the image of smallness in plain words. – "pigmy" is dated and, as a word for people, now offensive; the image is kept.

8. ch32-p001 – "To perpetuate it would be … “to decree universal mediocrity"." The source closes the quotation with a straight quote mark after an opening curly one. – Normalized to matching curly quotes. – Punctuation only; no change of reading.

9. ch32-p001 – Long appositive sentence "Its annihilation, the transformation…, of the pigmy property…, the expropriation of the great mass…, this fearful and painful expropriation … forms the prelude to the history of capital." – Split into three sentences ("Its destruction is the transformation…", "It is the expropriation…", "This terrible and painful expropriation … forms the prelude…"). Every item stays, and the identity of the annihilation with this expropriation is kept as statement of equivalence, not of cause. – §1.3 sentence length; no ambiguity was resolved by the split.

10. [^1] – Sismondi's French. – French kept exactly as printed, including "societé" and "a séparer" (missing accent). 11. ch32-p002 – "proletarians" in Marx's voice is the first use of "proletariat" in the chapter. – Added the §7 wording in parentheses with a grammatical adjustment: "proletarians (members of the proletariat, the class of wage workers, …)". – §7.0 allows grammatical adjustment.

12. ch32-p002 – "centralisation of capital" first appears here, and Marx explains it in the same passage ("One capitalist always kills many"; "this centralisation, or this expropriation of many capitalists by few"). – No §7 explanation added. – §7.0: Marx's own words serve as the definition.

13. ch32-p002 – "socially exploited and, therefore, common means of production": "exploited" here means "worked/used", not exploitation of workers, but Marx's word may also carry the second sense. – Kept "socially exploited" unchanged. – Ambiguity kept (§1.1).

14. ch32-p002 – "who usurp and monopolise" / p004 "usurpers". – Kept "usurp" and "usurpers" (not "seize"), since "seize" would drop the charge of illegitimacy and p004 turns on the word "usurpers". – Strength kept (§1.1).
