# Notes: ch24-s1.md

1. ch24-p001 – "accumulation of capital" is a §7 first-appearance term, but Marx defines it himself in this very sentence ("Employing surplus-value as capital, reconverting it into capital, is called accumulation of capital"). – Did not add the §7 explanation. Explained surplus-value and capital in a parenthesis between Marx's two sentences instead. – §7.0 "Never contradict the text": his words are the definition.

2. ch24-p002 – "240,000 lbs. of yarn annually, having a value of £2,000": the paragraph's own arithmetic (capital £10,000 + surplus-value £2,000; 40,000 lbs. = one-sixth = £2,000) requires £12,000 for the gross product. – Kept "£2,000" as printed. – Very probably a dropped digit, but changing a figure is "correcting" a fact (§1.4 forbids correcting arithmetic), and the script checks figures; logged for the reviewer.

3. ch24-p002 – First technical uses of wages, value, and rate of surplus-value all fall in this paragraph. – Wages explained in a parenthesis at the word; value and rate of surplus-value each in a parenthetical sentence right after their sentence. In the rate-of-surplus-value explanation, "Marx also calls it" became "it is also called" because the text is in Marx's own voice. "(s/v)" was left out of that explanation to avoid adding a formula the original paragraph does not contain. Marx's participial "The rate of surplus-value being 100%, the surplus-value lies…" became "The rate … is 100%. … At this rate, the surplus-value lies…", which keeps the dependence without adding a stronger "therefore". – §7.0, §1.1.

4. ch24-p005 – "advanced in the money form": here "money form" means only that the capital existed as money, not the §7 sense (the form of value in which gold is universal equivalent). – No §7 explanation; "money form" stays as printed. – §7.0: explain only at a technical use; the context file matches words, not senses.

5. ch24-p006 – "of which each capitalist had in hand only an aliquot part". The §3.3 rendering "equal fraction" would make the sentence say that every capitalist holds an equal share of the annual product, a claim Marx does not make. – Rendered "only a fractional part". – Fidelity outranks the (non-enforced) glossary rendering (§1.1); logged for the reviewer.

6. ch24-p008 – "means of production" and "means of subsistence" are first-appearance terms, but Marx glosses both himself here ("articles as can be employed in the labour process (i.e., means of production)", "articles … suitable for the sustenance of the labourer (i.e., means of subsistence)"). – Kept his identifications as the definitions and added no §7 explanations for these two; added the §7 explanation only for "labor process" (the term his gloss rests on) and for "surplus labor". – §7.0 "Never contradict the text".

7. ch24-p009 / p001 / p002 – The §7 explanations of surplus-value (p001) and wages (p002) mention labor-power before its own first technical use in Marx's text (p009). – Gave the full labor-power explanation at p009, its first use in Marx's voice. – §7 explanations are used verbatim; reordering them would require extending them.

8. ch24-p013 – "Though the latter with a portion of that tribute purchases the additional labour-power even at its full price … yet the transaction is … only the old dodge". "Though" can be read as factual ("although it does") or concessive ("even if"). – Kept one concessive sentence beginning "Though…" so both readings remain; did not split it. Price explained in a parenthesis after the sentence. "old dodge" → "old trick". – §0.6 ambiguity beats sentence length.

9. ch24-p015 – Only "living … labour" appears (with "past unpaid labour" as its opposite); the §7 entry is the pair "dead labor / living labor". – Explained living labor and named dead labor in the same parenthesis, using the §7 wording. – §7.0, grammatical adjustment only.

10. ch24-p016 – The long "in so far as … ; in so far as … ; in so far as all this is true, it is evident that …" sentence. – Kept it as one sentence with the four "insofar as" clauses, because splitting them into flat statements would turn Marx's conditions into assertions. "it is evident that" → "clearly" (§3.3). "alienating his own commodities" → "parting with his own commodities"; likewise "alienated" → "handed over" in p018 and p020. – §0.6; §1.1 keep strength.

11. ch24-p028 – "It is indeed notorious that … primogeniture works miracles." – Kept "notorious" (the irony depends on it); added a neutral identification "(the eldest son's right to inherit)" because the joke cannot be followed without knowing the word. – §1.1 (c), once in the chapter.

12. ch24-p029 – "consuming only a portion of it and converting the rest into money": one might expect "into capital". – Kept "money" as printed. – Not a one-reading typo (§1.4); changing it would alter the claim.

13. ch24-p030 – The paragraph states the commodity-production view ("The surplus-value is his property; it has never belonged to anyone else…") without a tag, as the original does. – Added no attribution and no hedge; Marx's interjections "(should read: …)" inside the Sismondi quotation take American spelling (labor), the quoted words keep "labour". Added "[the master]" after *(le maître)*. – §0.9, §1.2, §2.3.
