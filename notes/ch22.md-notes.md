# Notes for ch22.md

1. ch22-p001 – Many §7 terms first appear in this one opening paragraph (labor-power, value of labor-power, means of subsistence, surplus-value, wages, working day, time wages / piece wages, productivity of labor, intensity of labor). – Gave each §7 explanation at its first technical use, splitting some into short separate sentences after the term rather than stacking parentheses inside Marx's sentence. – §7.0; keeps Marx's own sentences readable and his argument steps in order.

2. ch22-p001 – "the productiveness of labour, its extensive and intensive magnitude": "its" most naturally means labor's, but could be read as referring to productiveness. – Kept "the productivity of labor; and its extensive and intensive magnitude" with the same "its", and put the productivity explanation at the paragraph's last sentence so that the possessive stays exactly as Marx wrote it. – Keep ambiguity (§1.1).

3. ch22-p001 – "the price of labour" (realized in means of subsistence) and "the value, or respectively of the price, of labour-power". – Kept "price of labor" exactly (an expression Marx calls irrational); rendered "respectively" as "or its price, as the case may be". "exoteric form of wages" → "outward form of wages". – §3.1 note on price/value of labor.

4. ch22-p001 – "magnitude of the value of labour-power": "magnitude of value" is not in the context file's list and is not used here in the commodity sense. – Kept "magnitude" without a §7 explanation. – Glossary keeps "magnitude"; the surrounding text ("absolutely or relatively", "amount") makes the sense clear.

5. ch22-p002 – "the measure of value by the mere duration of the working-time" uses "measure of value" in the sense of measuring value by labor-time, not money's function as measure of value (§7.1). – Did not add the §7 "measure of value" explanation (it would contradict the text); rendered "the measuring of value by the mere length of the working time" and gave the §7 explanation of "value" here instead. – §7.0 first technical use only / never contradict the text.

6. ch22-p002 – Original says "socially necessary time", not "socially necessary labour-time". – Kept "socially necessary time" in Marx's sentence and added the §7 explanation as a following sentence naming "socially necessary labor-time". – Wording kept; explanation given at first technical use.

7. ch22-p002 – "universal market" → "world market"; "universal labour" kept as "universal labor". – "universal market" is the same thing Marx calls the "world-market" in p003; "universal labour" is the unit of a scale and any paraphrase ("labor worldwide") would interpret it. – Plainness vs. meaning risk; kept the term where it is conceptual.

8. ch22-p004 – "capital" has no clean first technical use before the punch-line "capital itself" in p016, where an explanation would kill the irony. – Gave the §7 "capital" explanation as a short sentence after the first "capitalist production" in p004. – §7.0; irony in p016 kept intact (§1.2).

9. ch22-p004 – "price" first appears technically in p001 as the (irrational) "price of labour" / price of labor-power. – Gave the §7 "price" explanation (money name of the value embodied in a commodity) in p004, where commodity prices first appear. – Putting the commodity definition onto "price of labor" in p001 would blur Marx's distinction.

10. ch22-p009 – "Russian manufacture" means Russian manufacturing industry in general (cotton factories), not the manufacture period/form of Part 4. – Kept the word "manufacture" (glossary) but added no §7 explanation. – §7.0: everyday sense, not the technical one.

11. ch22-p005 – "daily or weekly, &tc., wage" in Marx's voice. – Rendered "etc." – §3.4; "&tc." treated as a variant of "&c.".

12. ch22-p005, [^3] – "&tc.," after the closing quote of Anderson's title. – Expanded to "etc." (outside the title's quotation marks); "Edin." kept as part of the citation. – §2.6 expands "&c." outside titles. Logged because "&tc." is a nonstandard form.

13. ch22-p007, [^4] – "op. cit." is not in the §2.6/§3.4 list. – Expanded to "cited above", the same rendering as "l.c.", since it means the same thing. – Consistency with §2.6; citation details (author, page) unchanged.

14. ch22-p008 – "Wages are much lower than in England, in many cases 50%": unclear whether 50% lower or 50% of English wages; also unclear whether this sentence is still the manager's report or Redgrave's/Marx's summary. – Kept "in many cases 50%" without adding "by", and did not attach a "he declares" tag to that sentence. – Keep ambiguity (§1.1). The second sentence ("He also declares that the workers…") repeats the tag only because it continues the original "declares that…and that" clause.

15. ch22-p014 – Redgrave's quotation (evidence). – Kept near-verbatim; glossed "minded [tended]" and "turning off [producing]"; "unfavorable" was already so spelled in the source. Marx's interjection "(whilst in the table the weavers are not deducted)" put in his plain voice: "(while in the table the weavers are not subtracted)". – §2.1, §0.9.

16. ch22-p016 – "For State intervention has falsified the natural economic relations. The different national wages must be reckoned, therefore, as if…" is Carey's position in free indirect speech. – Added "he says" and "according to him" so it cannot read as Marx's own claim. – §1.2 free indirect speech.

17. ch22-p016 – "in order afterwards to discover" is ironic (as if the first declaration were made for the sake of the later discovery). – Kept the purpose construction: "who did so in order to discover afterwards". – Irony kept (§1.2), no "only" added.

18. ch22-p016 – "are not the ideal product of the real economic movement": "ideal" today suggests "perfect". – Rendered "are not the product, in the realm of ideas, of the real economic movement". – Meaning risk: "ideal" here means "in thought"; the rendering states that sense without adding interpretation.

19. ch22-p016 – "the System of Protection" may be unknown to a modern reader. – Added the neutral identification "(protective tariffs)" once. – §1.1 (c). "commerce" → "trade" (§3.3); "spurious erudition" → "fake learning"; "want of the critical faculty" → "lack of the critical faculty".
