# Notes for ch13b.md

1. ch13-p012, ch13-p015 – "concentration of capital" was listed as a first appearance. – Did NOT add the §7 explanation. Both uses are "concentration of the means of production" (gathering means of production in one place / in one capitalist's hands), which Marx explains himself in p015. The §7 wording ("growth of individual capitals through accumulation") would add a mechanism this passage does not state. – §7.0 first *technical* use and "never contradict the text"; the explanation belongs in Ch. 25. Reviewer may disagree.

2. ch13-p014 – §7 explanations for "wages" and "means of subsistence" added here, at their first technical use. "Wages" went in as a separate bracketed sentence after the sentence that equates "the total value of these labour-powers" with "the amount of the wages", so the added explanation does not alter Marx's own equation. "Means of subsistence" explained with "that is, over the food, clothing, housing, and other necessities they need to live".

3. ch13-p013 – One very long "Whether … or … or …" sentence listing nine possible causes. – Split into a lead sentence ("may gain this increased productive power for any of several reasons") and nine "It may …" sentences, then "Whichever of these is the cause…". – "may" keeps the alternatives as possibilities, not as claims that all apply in every case. The original has a missing dash before "whichever"; the split makes that moot (no correction needed).

4. ch13-p020 – "If, then, the control of the capitalist is in substance two-fold by reason of the two-fold nature of the process of production itself, which, on the one hand, is … on the other, … in form that control is despotic." – Moved the relative clause about the two-fold nature of production into its own sentence before the "If" clause, and kept "If, then, … in substance … in form … despotic". – The relative clause is asserted in the original anyway, so stating it separately adds no claim; the conditional structure and the substance/form contrast are kept.

5. ch13-p020 – *faux frais* in Marx's voice → "overhead costs" per §3.4 (not quoted). "overlookers" → "overseers" (§3.3).

6. ch13-p016 – "small master" → "small employer" (§3.3 masters → employers; "small employers" is also Marx's own phrase in p026).

7. ch13-p022, p024 – "ancient Asiatics" / "Asiatic … kings" in Marx's voice → "ancient Asian peoples" / "Asian … kings" (§4 outdated names, neutral modern name). "Oriental States" and "Asiatic monarchy" inside the Jones quotation (p023) kept as printed. "Etruscan theocrats" → "Etruscan priest-rulers" (plain sense of the word).

8. ch13-p023 – Jones quotation kept near-verbatim. One sentence split at "utility" (§2.1). "prodigally" glossed as "prodigally [lavishly]". The stray comma in "human labour almost alone, was prodigally used" kept as printed (inside a quotation; not a clear one-reading typo).

9. ch13-p025 – "races who live by the chase" → "peoples who live by hunting"; "navel-string" → "umbilical cord" (image kept). "Indian communities" kept as printed without identification (in context it is the village communities of India; adding "of India" was judged unnecessary to follow the passage, §1.1 c).

10. [^20] – "Linguet is improbably right" – Kept as printed. Other editions read "probably right", but "improbably" has a possible (if odd) reading ("surprisingly right"), so it is not a one-reading typo (§1.4). Added the bracketed translation [Theory of Civil Laws] for the French title.

11. [^13] – Italian (Carli) translated literally. "fino a che" rendered "to the point that"; it can also mean "as long as" / "until". Safest literal gloss chosen and logged per §0.11. "diminuere" kept as printed.

14. ch13-p017 – "formal result" / "real requisite" kept as "formal" / "real", since Marx is contrasting formal and real subjection of labor to capital; not explained further.
