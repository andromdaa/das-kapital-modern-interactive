[^75] – "measure of value" is listed as a first appearance in Ch. 15, but here it means labor-time as the measure of value, not money's function of expressing values in gold. – No §7 explanation added. – The §7 wording ("money's function of expressing the values of all commodities as amounts of gold") would contradict the text (§7.0: technical sense only; never contradict the text).

2. ch15-p071 – "intensity of labor": first appearance in the chapter. Marx develops the idea himself in p072 ("condensation", "density"), but p071 only names it. – Added the §7 explanation as a short parenthetical sentence right after "took its intensity as given". – The §7 wording does not contradict p072's development, and p071 is where the reader first needs it.

3. ch15-p072 – "imposes on the workman increased expenditure of labour..., heightened tension of labour-power, and closer filling up of the pores of the working day, or condensation of labour to a degree..." – Kept as one sentence with "or condensation of labor" in the same place. – It is unclear whether "condensation" renames only the filling of the pores or all three items; splitting would settle it (§0.6).

4. ch15-p077 – "operative machines" (machines, not people). – Rendered "working machines". – The glossary's "operative → factory worker" applies to people; "working machine" is Marx's standard term (§3.1 machinery row). Script warning on p077 is expected.

5. ch15-p087 – Participial clause "being always estimated in the official returns according to the power of similar engines in 1828, is only nominal". – Rendered as a relative clause ("which the official returns always estimate...") followed by "is only nominal". – Avoided adding "so"/"therefore", which would make a causal link explicit that the participle leaves implicit.
