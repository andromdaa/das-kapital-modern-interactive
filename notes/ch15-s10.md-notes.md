# Notes for ch15-s10.md

1. ch15-p313 – "circulation (of commodities)" is listed as a first appearance in Ch. 15, but every "circulation" in this file is non-technical: "circulation of matter between man and the soil" (twice, Marx's metabolism image) and "circulation of air" (inside the Liebig quotation). – No §7 explanation added; "circulation of matter" kept word for word. – §7.0: explain only at the first technical use; adding "the exchange of commodities through money" would contradict the text.

2. ch15-p312 – "If the use of machinery … is free from the injurious physical effect …, its action … is more intense" is a concessive "if". – Kept it as one "If …, …" sentence instead of splitting into "X. But Y.", so the logical link stays exactly as printed. – Splitting would have forced a contrast or inference the original only implies.

3. ch15-p312 – "it is as yet only virtually that agricultural machines replace labourers" – Kept "only virtually" without a gloss. – Marx explains it himself in the next sentence ("in other words…").

4. ch15-p313 – "agriculture and manufacture in their infancy" and "In agriculture as in manufacture" – "manufacture" here means industry in general rather than the Part 4 period/form. – Kept the word "manufacture" (glossary rendering); no §7 explanation (already appeared earlier in the chapter, and this is not the technical sense anyway).

5. ch15-p313 – Typo/spacing: "[^246]Capitalist" (no space after the marker) and "wealth-the soil" (hyphen used as a dash). – Fixed to "[^246] Capitalist" and "wealth – the soil". – One-reading punctuation fixes (§1.4).

6. ch15-p313 – "the town operatives" in "concentration increases that of the town operatives". – Rendered "increases the power of resistance of the town factory workers": repeated the noun instead of "that of" for clarity, and "operatives" → "factory workers" per glossary.

7. ch15-p313 – "The more a country starts its development on the foundation of modern industry, like the United States, for example" – Moved "like the United States, for example" next to "a country" so it cannot be read as modifying "modern industry". – This is the only sensible reading of the original; no claim changed.

8. [^246] – "Apart from Liebig's wrong interpretation of the word "labour,"" – Mention, not use (§1.2): Marx is talking about the word itself. Kept "labour" in British spelling inside its quotation marks.

9.
